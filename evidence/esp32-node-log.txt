ESP32-S3 (N16R8) motor node, live verification excerpt
Captured on the real board over its serial port while the backend ran on the same network.
The Wi-Fi name is replaced. The vibration sensor was NOT yet attached at this point, which is why
the firmware reports "no sensor" and assumes the motor follows the relay.

--- board serial output ---

Not paired yet. In the Alexa app: Devices -> + -> Add Device -> Matter,
relay=OFF vib=0.0000 running=no (no sensor)
Backend: relay ON            <- command sent from the dashboard side (POST /devices/node-motor1/power {"power":"on"})
relay=ON vib=0.0000 running=yes (no sensor)
Backend: relay OFF           <- command sent from the dashboard side ({"power":"off"})

--- backend view ---
node-motor1 | ESP32 motor | power: off | online: True
POST /nodes/motor1/report -> 200   (repeated once per second while the board is on the network)

--- offline detection ---

a "not_responding" alert for node-motor1, as designed.
